0.6.0

Released · 9 Sept 2026

The seven currency marks arrive with circled halves, and those hold 1 unit of daylight against the ring rather than the 2 the guide asks between elements: a container is the frame a drawing sits in, not a neighbour it has to be told apart from, and at 2 a letterform comes out at about half the well. Bitcoin has no circled half, because its four stubs stand outside the letter at both ends and solving them shrinks the B until its bowls carry 1 unit of white instead of 2.

29 drawings added since 0.5.0, bringing the set to 692. Every drawing of this release is on keylineicons.com/changelog.

0.5.0

Released · 8 Sept 2026

Two names changed hands: the diagonal chain is link now and the horizontal one link-2, which is the naming the rest of the set already follows, and link-off became link-2-off with it, so an import of either wants updating. Four sharp drawings that painted outside their rounded siblings were re-solved rather than exempted, and the formatting marks moved onto a Text shelf of their own.

35 drawings added since 0.4.0, bringing the set to 663, and 7 redrawn. Every drawing of this release is on keylineicons.com/changelog.

0.4.0

Released · 6 Sept 2026

Every sharp diagonal end is cut back to its round cap's box, on 329 icons: the chevrons, arrowheads, signs and slashes that painted 0.414 outside their rounded siblings sit on the same box now, and every arc the converter had bent by moving its endpoint is a true arc again. settings-dot's sharp gear is cut on its cap's own line, so its grey plate ends flush with the black instead of escaping past it, and circle-navigation sits centred in its box.

44 drawings added since 0.3.0, bringing the set to 629, and 315 redrawn. Every drawing of this release is on keylineicons.com/changelog.
